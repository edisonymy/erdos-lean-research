# Recency and collision audit — 2 August 2026

This is a search record, not proof of novelty and not evidence about private
or unindexed work.

- The live [Erdos Problems #742](https://www.erdosproblems.com/742) entry still
  labels the problem `DECIDABLE`, meaning reduced to a finite check rather than
  solved, and reports no claimed proof.
- Exact-title, problem-number, and keyword searches covered arXiv, Zenodo,
  GitHub, the Erdős Problems forum/index, and the general web. Queries combined
  `Erdos 742`, `Murty-Simon`, `diameter-2-critical`, `order 25`, `SAT`, `UNSAT`,
  `certificate`, `automorphism`, and `fixed vertices`.
- The pinned Formal Conjectures material concerns formalization of the
  statement, not a proof. Brian Li's public `diameter2critical` repository
  supplies CNF instances but no matching fixed-15 certificate verdict.
- The current literature hits were the known general and restricted results,
  including Fan's finite reduction, Füredi's sufficiently-large-order theorem,
  and later structural special cases. None reported this exact split-case
  computation.

No public full proof, counterexample, certificate-backed order-25 closure, or
matching `1^15 5^2`, `t=57` exclusion was found in the repeated search. The
public claim is deliberately limited to the hash-locked computation and makes
no absolute priority claim.
