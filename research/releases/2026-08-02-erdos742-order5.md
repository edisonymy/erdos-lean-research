# Certificate-backed order-five symmetry exclusion for Erdős #742

Published: 2 August 2026

## Audited result

Conditional on Fan's order-25 numerical bound, the published maximum-degree
reduction of Haynes--Henning--van der Merwe--Yeo, and the published
dominating-edge case of Dailly--Foucaud--Hansberg, no 25-vertex Murty--Simon
counterexample has an automorphism of order five with exactly five fixed
vertices (cycle type `1^5 5^4`).

This is a restricted computational theorem. It does **not** solve Erdős
Problem #742 and does not close the unrestricted order-25 case.

## Verification record

Orbit arithmetic reduces the claim to six fixed-vertex graph types. For every
type, a definition-level quotient CNF was regenerated byte-for-byte and its
retained LRAT certificate was accepted by the independently compiled,
hash-pinned `lrat-check` executable. A quotient-specific exhaustive audit
compared the encoding with direct diameter and edge-deletion checks on 135,468
small invariant graphs and found zero mismatches.

The package contains:

- the exact generator and reduction audits;
- six compressed LRAT certificates and SHA-256 manifests;
- CaDiCaL, `drat-trim`, and `lrat-check` logs;
- a clean replay script; and
- a current-through-2-August-2026 collision audit.

The LRATs certify the generated CNFs. The mathematical conclusion additionally
depends on the cited published reductions, the audited encoding and symmetry
arguments, and the native proof checker. There is no Lean formalization of this
restricted theorem yet.

## Secondary order-32 frontier for Erdős #64

The same publication wave records a cautious trusted-solver frontier for #64.
SMS/Glasgow excludes the Carr-structured order-32 degree families with
`h = 8, 10, 12` independent degree-four vertices and all other vertices cubic.
The `h = 4, 6` searches timed out, the unrestricted search found no witness but
did not finish, and no LRAT certificates were produced. These are bounded
family computations only.
