# Certificate-backed second order-five symmetry exclusion for Erdős #742

Published: 2 August 2026

## Audited result

Conditional on Fan's order-25 numerical bound, the published maximum-degree
reduction of Haynes--Henning--van der Merwe--Yeo, and the published
dominating-edge case of Dailly--Foucaud--Hansberg, no 25-vertex Murty--Simon
counterexample has an automorphism of order five with exactly ten fixed
vertices (cycle type `1^10 5^3`).

Combined with the earlier certificate package, counterexamples with exactly
five or exactly ten fixed vertices are excluded. Fixed-15, fixed-20, and
asymmetric cases remain. This is a restricted computational theorem, not a
solution of Erdős Problem #742 and not a Lean proof.

## Verification record

The hash-locked quotient CNF has 56,164 variables and 240,029 clauses and
includes all nine feasible weighted edge counts. An independent audit checked
the full centralizer generators and compared quotient semantics with direct
graph definitions on 28,039 graphs, finding zero mismatches.

CaDiCaL 1.9.5 returned UNSAT. `drat-trim` verified the 419,261,312-byte DRAT
and converted it to LRAT; the independently compiled native `lrat-check` then
reported `c VERIFIED` on the 2,193,318,873-byte LRAT. Exact SHA-256 hashes are
in `MANIFEST.json`. The CNF, 407,165,840-byte compressed LRAT, build metadata,
and full solver/checker logs are attached as release assets.

The certificate proves the generated CNF is UNSAT. The graph-theoretic result
also depends on the cited reductions, the audited quotient encoding and
symmetry argument, and the native checker. The announcement-level collision
search found no matching public result, but no absolute priority claim is made.
