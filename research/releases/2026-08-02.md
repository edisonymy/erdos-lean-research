# Audited research wave — 2 August 2026

This is a timestamped release of reproducible partial results from the
Erdős–Lean research campaign. **No open Erdős problem is claimed solved.**

## Main mathematical artifacts

- **Erdős #128 (sparse halves).** The order-16 case is excluded, conditional
  on the completeness of the cited McKay Ramsey catalogue. Independent SAT
  encodings and checked LRAT certificates were subsequently replaced in the
  final residual cases by a short combinatorial argument. All-order reductions
  show that a smallest counterexample would have even order, minimum degree
  `> 1 + 2(n-1)/25`, and independence ratio strictly between
  `(33-sqrt(161))/116` and `2/5`; the lower endpoint is a consequence of
  Razborov's published theorem, not a novelty claim. In the lower part of this
  window, the campaign proves an additional low-cross-degree constraint.
- **Erdős #167 (Tuza).** An exact, independently replayed exclusion through
  order 11, conditional on Puleo's reduction and nauty catalogue completeness.
- **Erdős #276 (Fibonacci-type recurrence).** A definition-level audit verifies
  the published finite covering construction and isolates the genuinely
  unresolved escape condition. Several tempting proof shortcuts are disproved
  by exact arithmetic checks; this is a negative research package.
- **Erdős #366 and #982.** Exact exclusions for explicitly stated restricted
  construction families, with independent checkers. These do not settle the
  unrestricted problems.
