# Erdős #151 allocation checkpoint

**Recorded:** 2 August 2026.  **Purpose:** guard the full-resolution campaign
against converting genuine momentum into an open-ended #151 rabbit hole.
This is an evidence-based checkpoint, not an irrevocable schedule.  Future
campaign decisions may override it when new evidence changes the expected
probability of a full resolution per unit of time.

## Current judgement

#151 has earned one more concentrated cycle.  The reasons are unusually
concrete:

- a verified order-41 counterexample with `beta<=9` would settle the full
  conjecture negatively, not merely improve a bound;
- the order-41 `omega=5` lane has now collapsed to a short theorem conditional
  only on completeness of the published seven-record Ramsey `(3,6;17)`
  catalogue;
- the sole remaining `omega=4` obstruction has a realized four-residual local
  abstraction, so a global completion search starts from an actual near-model
  rather than an empty encoding;
- Erdős's recorded doubt gives the counterexample direction a materially
  better prior than a typical famous conjecture.

These facts justify momentum, but they do not justify indefinite depth.  The
local abstraction still has independence number 15, far from the required
global bound nine, and the all-order positive theorem runs into unresolved
Ramsey numbers and sparse-Ramsey constants.  Those are warning signs, not
minor technicalities.

A parallel outside-option scan found no untouched target that presently
clears the one-CPU-day/full-resolution gate.  The three closest non-promotions
(#701, #149, and #778) failed for recorded finite-coverage, structural, or
collision reasons; see
[`../full_solution_scout/triage-2026-08-02/OUTSIDE_OPTION_VALUE.md`](../full_solution_scout/triage-2026-08-02/OUTSIDE_OPTION_VALUE.md).
This comparison supports one more #151 cycle, but it must be refreshed at the
checkpoint rather than treated as a permanent ranking.

## Next allocation cycle

Until the next checkpoint, allocate at most:

1. one long-running lane to the **global order-41 `omega=4` counterexample
   search**, including joint cross-fan compatibility and the true global
   `beta<=9` condition;
2. one mathematical/audit lane to constraints that directly cut that global
   model or verify a candidate;
3. publication effort for the completed `omega=5` theorem; and
4. at least one independent scouting/probe lane outside #151 whenever agent
   or compute capacity permits.

The checkpoint occurs after the first genuinely global `omega=4` model run
finishes, or after roughly 12 wall-clock hours, whichever comes first.  A
future agent should record the observed solver/model statistics rather than
renewing the siege from narrative momentum alone.

## Evidence that earns another cycle

Continue or expand #151 only if at least one of these occurs:

- a definition-level verified counterexample candidate, even if a secondary
  verification layer remains;
- a globally compatible model with `omega=4` and `alpha<=9` whose remaining
  `beta` violations are few and shrink under CEGAR;
- a certified exhaustion that removes a substantial fraction of the 29
  conservation profiles, with a credible path to all remaining profiles;
- a new lemma that acts simultaneously on all four residuals and eliminates
  at least one complete conservation profile or another globally defined
  family, with a measured reduction in the production search; ruling out one
  chosen local witness does not qualify;
- an unconditional replacement for the order-17 catalogue premise; or
- a genuinely uniform argument that advances every Ramsey jump, rather than
  another isolated finite order.

Any one of these is a real signal.  Extend by one checkpoint, then reassess.

## Evidence to reallocate away

Reduce #151 to a maintenance/review lane and move most resources back to the
full target-acquisition pool if any two of the following hold at a checkpoint:

- new work produces only local structure, bounded cases, or sharper constants
  without changing the global model;
- two materially different global encodings find the same broad feasible
  region but no near-counterexample and no certificate-sized exclusion;
- a complete profile run is projected to exceed roughly 48 hours without a
  proof-producing solver path or useful intermediate certificate;
- the observed CEGAR loop keeps adding unrelated bad ten-sets without a
  falling violation count or reusable separation pattern;
- the positive/general route again depends on unknown `R(3,h)` values or
  asymptotic sparse-Ramsey constants before it reaches a uniform theorem;
- independent literature or expert review materially lowers novelty or shows
  that the apparent frontier is already covered.

Reallocate immediately, without waiting for two signals, if a prior complete
solution is found, the statement-to-model audit fails, or a claimed candidate
fails either independent checker.

On reallocation, retain approximately 15--25% effort for #151 publication,
expert feedback, and inexpensive follow-ups; use the rest for Tier-1/2
counterexample-first targets.  This preserves option value without allowing
sunk cost to dictate the portfolio.

## Decision principle

The question is not whether #151 remains interesting or whether another lemma
is available.  It is whether the next unit of agent/solver time has a better
chance of producing a **full, first, creditable resolution** than the best
available alternative target.  Momentum counts only insofar as it changes
that probability.

## Corrected finite-results checkpoint: 3 August 2026, 16:10 BST

- The chromatic argument inherited from the symbolic-h lane was audited and
  corrected.  Lovasz decomposition plus Brooks gives the valid pure-triangular
  gate

  ```text
  beta(G) <= h-1  =>  n <= (h-1) 3 ceil(h/4) / 2.
  ```

  It unconditionally excludes pure-triangular jump witnesses for `h=11,12`
  using the April 2026 Ramsey bounds.  A primary-source check of the stronger
  Borodin--Kostochka/Catlin bound `chi<=floor(3(Delta+2)/4)` also closes the
  pure-triangular `h=13` jump face, but leaves possible `h=14` jump orders
  67--71.  The advertised all-large-order tail, unconditional `h=15,16`
  consequences, and claim that an upper Ramsey bound closes `h=14` were false
  and are withdrawn.
  The useful order-50 strengthening is instead finite: for a `K4`-free graph
  on `n` vertices with `beta<=b`, its ambient-maximal-edge graph `M` satisfies

  ```text
  nu(M) >= ceil((n-P_q(b))/2),
  q=3 ceil((b+1)/4),  P_q(b)=b+(q-2) floor(b/2).
  ```

  Hence every `(n,b)=(50,10)` witness has three pairwise disjoint maximal
  edges.  A separately audited successor CEGAR encoding now contains exactly
  this gate; it is live and has no candidate or UNSAT result.

## Global-separator successor: 3 August 2026, 16:20 BST

Fable's V1 observation survived an independent semantics audit and has been
converted into a separate successor rather than injected into a live formula.
If `V(G)=A union B` with both induced sides triangle-free, coloring crossing
edges red and internal edges blue gives a two-edge-coloring with no
monochromatic triangle.  Therefore an edge-arrowing target admits no such
partition.  For every rejected model, the sound lazy clause is the disjunction
of triangle witnesses over triples internal to `A` or to `B`.

The standalone separator agrees with brute force on all 32,768 labeled
six-vertex graphs, and 16,384 fixed graph/partition tests verify that the cut
has exactly the intended semantics with the inherited one-way triangle
witnesses.  The combined matching-three/TCG-3 integration audit is `PASS`;
matched `(10,4)` controls reached UNSAT in 4, 4, and 3 rounds versus 18, 21,
and 18 for matching-three alone.  These are regression controls, not evidence
about order 50.

A third, independent order-50 production run is now live.  Its static formula
is exactly the audited matching-three formula; TCG-3 contributes only sound
model-dependent cuts.  The first 780 sampled models all admitted a
triangle-free two-partition and were separated, while the admissible-set
oracle also continued to return its full 24-cut batch.  This is useful initial
telemetry because the new global oracle is active, but it is not evidence for
SAT, UNSAT, or proximity.  The inherited and matching-three-only runs remain
live because stopping them would discard unpersisted learned clauses; no such
destructive scheduler change was made.
