# Erdős #203 bounded attack: exact results and stop record

**Date:** 3 August 2026
**Status:** stopped after independent compute, algebraic, cover-theory, and
non-affine gates; **no solution claimed**.

## Problem and certificate route

Erdős #203 asks whether there is an integer `m >= 1`, coprime to 6, for
which every

```text
2^k 3^l m + 1,  k,l >= 0,
```

is composite.  A finite cover of the exponent lattice by prime-divisibility
fibres would resolve the problem affirmatively: for a prime `q > 3`, one
chosen fibre of the homomorphism

```text
(k,l) -> 2^k 3^l in <2,3> <= F_q^*
```

fixes one congruence class for `m modulo q`; the Chinese remainder theorem
then combines the classes.  An independently checked full cover and a CRT
representative larger than every certificate prime would be a short,
mechanically verifiable complete solution.  None was found.

## Exact results

Three independent lanes produced the following reusable results.

1. **Complete small-image-order census.**  Factoring and reconstructing
   `gcd(2^r-1,3^r-1)` for every `1 <= r <= 1000` finds exactly 238 primes for
   which `<2,3>` has order at most 1000.  Union with the legacy
   period-720720 pool gives 281 distinct maps: 182 more maps and
   `0.593728525078175` more reciprocal density than the legacy pool.  An
   independent prime scan through the largest census prime, 67,033, agrees.

2. **Exact algebraic-cell classification.**  For the ansatz `m=A^R`,
   Capell's binomial criterion leaves precisely the odd-prime cells
   `k=l=0 (mod p)` for odd `p|R`, plus the Sophie--Germain cell
   `k=2 (mod 4), l=0 (mod 4)` when `4|R`.  There are no further
   cyclotomic/Aurifeuilian cells for `2^k 3^l A^R+1`.  The exact CRT phase
   condition is

   ```text
   gcd(R,q-1) | ((q-1)/2 - d_q t).
   ```

   The finite audit replays 204 polynomial tests, 26,224 phase tests, 10,619
   modular-root tests, and twelve exact density checks.

3. **Limits of the natural power hierarchy.**  All odd-power automatic cells
   together have density at most `1-8/pi^2 = 0.1894305...`.  For the audited
   281-map pool, an exact rational calculation rules out the natural
   common-shear lift of a one-dimensional Sierpiński cover: after reducing the
   shear modulo 12, the largest compatible reciprocal density is
   `0.6724974471192757 < 1`, attained at residue 7.

## Decisive negative evidence for these lanes

The expanded pool initially left 6.756% of a 50,000-point training sample
uncovered, but this was sample overfitting.  A fixed refined phase vector left
10.9900%, 11.0360%, and 10.9804% uncovered on three fresh 250,000-point
samples.  The best tested perfect-power variants were worse, and the residual
failed the predeclared sub-5% continuation gate.  These percentages are
heuristics, not lower bounds and not evidence that #203 has a negative answer.

Two public-record corrections are preserved:

- ten alleged new primes in a public partial computation are explicit
  semiprimes;
- the inherited claim of a roughly 72% proved ILP ceiling was not certified:
  the CBC run established neither feasibility nor infeasibility, so its
  status was UNKNOWN.

As of the dated recency audit, the live problem page still lists #203 as open
and identifies active workers.  A May 2026 purported Lean resolution assumes
the desired prime-production statement as an axiom.  The 24 July 2026
`vela-verify` release contains a verifier explicitly labelled for a **partial**
CRT cover.  A 3 August audit of the associated `vela-science/vela` and
`vela-science/erdos-frontier` repositories found one 20-row witness, with
`m=8168305011630835886634520238999`; its own retained claim records only about
0.7467 density and explicitly says the rows do not cover all residues.  The
current Observatory target reports no claim and asks for a full-cover
extension.  Moreover, the first uncovered point `(k,l)=(1,8)` gives

```text
2 * 3^8 * m + 1 = 107184498362619828504418174576144879,
```

which is prime; a dependency-free checker verifies a complete 25-node
recursive Lucas certificate.  Thus the displayed modulus is definitely not a
solution.  Database status is not treated as proof of novelty; repeat the
announcement and literature audit before any future claim.

The final structural pass also proves that every argument using finitely many
fixed divisors—whether presented as prime powers, composite moduli,
interacting congruences, or finite Boolean cases—collapses to the same affine
fibres.  Unequal rectangular periods produce no binomial factorisation cells
beyond the perfect-power and Sophie--Germain cases.  This does not prove that
every possible #203 solution has a finite cover: an infinite-prime solution or
a genuinely variable-divisor/global obstruction remains logically possible.

## Allocation decision

Do not enlarge the image-order bound, repeat flat phase descent, try more
perfect-power exponents, or vary the common-shear construction.  Renew #203
only for one of the following concrete signals:

- a genuinely new non-affine identity or recursive covering mechanism;
- a materially larger public prime-map pool whose fixed-vector validation
  passes the same cross-seed gate;
- an exact full cover, with independent cover and CRT verification.

Absent such a signal, further #203 work has lower one-week full-resolution
value than acquiring a new finite counterexample target.
