# Three exact small cases of Erdős #561

Date: 2026-08-03

## Result

The bounded counterexample search became a positive finite result.  Combining
complete lower-bound enumerations with the standard star-union upper
construction gives the following three exact values:

\[
\widehat r(K_{1,2}\sqcup K_{1,1},\;2K_{1,2})=8,
\]

\[
\widehat r(K_{1,2}\sqcup K_{1,1},\;K_{1,3}\sqcup K_{1,2})=9,
\]

and

\[
\widehat r(2K_{1,2},\;K_{1,3}\sqcup K_{1,1})=10.
\]

These are exact, computation-assisted determinations for three individual
target pairs.  They verify the Burr--Erdős--Faudree--Rousseau--Schelp
formula at these parameters.  They do **not** prove the general conjecture,
do **not** resolve Erdős problem #561, and provide no counterexample.

No earlier exact determination for these three pairs was found in the
primary-source and exact-notation priority audit.  That makes novelty
plausible, not guaranteed: a specialist/human literature check is still
required before a public novelty claim.

## Why the values are exact

For descending degree pairs `a=(a1,a2)` and `b=(b1,b2)`, put

\[
L=(a_1+b_1-1,\;\max(a_1+b_2-1,a_2+b_1-1),\;a_2+b_2-1).
\]

The three cases have respectively

| Red degrees | Blue degrees | `L` | Sum |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `(2,1)` | `(2,2)` | `(3,3,2)` | 8 |
| `(2,1)` | `(3,2)` | `(4,3,2)` | 9 |
| `(2,2)` | `(3,1)` | `(4,4,2)` | 10 |

The general upper-bound construction is the disjoint union of stars whose
degrees are the entries of `L`.  Thus it supplies hosts with 8, 9 and 10
edges.  `verify_formula_upper_hosts.py` also checks every coloring of those
three explicit hosts directly by non-induced graph monomorphism; all three
checks returned `VERIFIED`.

For the lower bounds, suppose a host has fewer than the claimed number of
edges.  Isolated vertices cannot participate in either target and may be
deleted.  A connected component with `e` edges has at most `e+1` vertices.
Consequently, all relevant connected components through nine edges are
generated by nauty `geng` on orders 2 through 10.  Every isolate-free host is
then a unique multiset of these connected component types.  The exact counts
are:

| Edges | Connected types | All isolate-free host types |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 1 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 | 5 |
| 4 | 5 | 11 |
| 5 | 12 | 26 |
| 6 | 30 | 68 |
| 7 | 79 | 177 |
| 8 | 227 | 497 |
| 9 | 710 | 1,476 |

Thus the cumulative lower-bound catalogues contain 290 hosts through seven
edges, 787 through eight edges, and 2,263 through nine edges.  Every host has
a saved red/blue coloring avoiding the corresponding red and blue target.
There is therefore no smaller arrowing host.

## Independent checks

The formula-8 case was generated first with the complete NetworkX atlas
through seven vertices plus all 23 nonisomorphic eight-vertex trees.  Its
hand-written edge-pattern detector checked all colorings.  An independent
audit then:

1. regenerated the connected catalogue with nauty `geng`;
2. independently reconstructed all component multisets;
3. obtained exactly the same connected and host counts; and
4. checked all 290 saved avoiding colorings using NetworkX VF2 non-induced
   monomorphism rather than the search detector.

It returned `VERIFIED`, with zero coloring failures.

The capped meta-sweep generated one shared nauty catalogue through nine
edges and used a general, hand-written star-embedding mask engine.  A separate
VF2 audit checked every saved witness: 787 colorings for the formula-9 case
and 2,263 for the formula-10 case, with zero failures and exact count/hash
agreement.  The nauty executable hash and raw graph6-stream hash are recorded
in `meta_sweep_result.json`.

## Priority and theorem-family audit

`PRIORITY_GATE.md` records the source-level audit.  In brief, none of the
three cases lies in the surviving exact families:

* neither the original both-uniform theorem nor the all-odd theorem applies;
* neither side is wholly a matching;
* Cheng's Theorem 18 fails because the two tails are not both 1;
* the Győri--Schelp strict numerical condition fails;
* the 2025 two-equal-stars theorem requires every degree on the other side
  to be at least 2, which fails in the two cases having a uniform forest;
* the remaining 2025 hypotheses do not apply; and
* Fu--Luo--Ni's arbitrary-forest claim appeared only in arXiv v1 and was
  removed wholesale in v2; current v3 concerns uniform star forests.

The live Erdős page was checked, but database status was not treated as
decisive.  Exact-expression searches, dissertations, the current paper
versions, the 1981 source, the 2025 version of record, GitHub, and adjacent
literature were inspected.  The nearest exact-looking search hit concerns a
connected size-Ramsey parameter and different targets.
