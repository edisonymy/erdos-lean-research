# A certified part-size bound for bipartite Erdős--Gyárfás counterexamples

Date: 2026-08-03/04 UTC

Status: **PROVED, subject to the explicitly stated computational
certificates.**  This is a restricted-class theorem and is not a resolution
of Erdős problem #64.  A targeted search found no prior statement of the
part-size bound, but novelty remains provisional pending specialist review.

## Theorem

Let `G=(X,Y;E)` be a finite simple bipartite graph of minimum degree at least
three.  If `G` contains no cycle of length 4, 8, or 16, then

```text
min(|X|,|Y|) >= 20.
```

Consequently every bipartite counterexample to the Erdős--Gyárfás
power-of-two-cycle conjecture has at least 20 vertices in each bipartition
class, and hence at least 40 vertices in total.

## Exact finite reduction

Choose one bipartition class `S`, of size `sigma`, as a point set.  Every
vertex on the other side is represented by its neighbourhood in `S`.
Minimum degree gives point-degrees at least three and hyperedge sizes at least
three.  The absence of `C4` makes the neighbourhood hypergraph linear: a pair
of points occurs in at most one hyperedge.  Therefore

```text
3m <= sum_A binom(|A|,2) <= binom(sigma,2),
```

where `m` is the number of hyperedges.  Thus
`m <= floor(binom(sigma,2)/3)`, giving a finite padded incidence matrix.
Cycles in the bipartite graph are exactly incidence cycles of this
hypergraph.

For `sigma <= 18`, the certificate ladder rules out the stronger class with
no `C4` and no `C8`, without needing the `C16` condition.  For `sigma = 19`,
the final formula encodes linearity, minimum point-degree and hyperedge size,
the exact `C8` obstruction, sound row/column symmetry breaking, and 512
independently audited clauses, each forbidding one explicit simple `C16`.
Every `C16`-free incidence structure satisfies every such clause regardless
of labeling.  The final formula is UNSAT, so no side-19 structure survives.
Applying the same argument to either bipartition class proves the theorem.

## Priority correction and current novelty status

The balanced 38-vertex equality graph for the weaker `{C4,C8}` problem is
not new.  The recovered model is exactly Gordon Royle's 2009 graph `F038A`,
the Levi graph of the cyclic `19_3` configuration generated by `{0,1,8}`.
The McKay--Afzaly exact extremal table already implies minimum *total* order
38 for the `{C4,C8}` class.  Those facts are cited and explicitly withdrawn
as novelty claims in the hostile audit.

The present theorem is different: it rules out an arbitrarily unbalanced
graph having either bipartition class of size at most 19 once `C16` is also
forbidden.  A current exact-phrase and citation-backtracking search found no
prior version of this part-size statement.  The nearest published
Erdős--Gyárfás result gives a total-order lower bound of 32 for bipartite
counterexamples.  Until configuration/census specialists confirm priority,
the result should be described as **certificate-backed and plausibly new**,
not unconditionally claimed as first.
