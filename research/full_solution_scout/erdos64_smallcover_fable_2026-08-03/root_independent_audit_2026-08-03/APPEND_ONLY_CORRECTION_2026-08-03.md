# Append-only correction to the #64 Fable packet

**Added:** 2026-08-03/04 UTC by an independent successor audit.

## Corrections

1. **The 38-vertex graph is prior, not new.**  The packet's
   `sigma19_model.json` is exactly isomorphic to Gordon Royle's graph
   posted on MathOverflow on 2 November 2009:
   <https://mathoverflow.net/questions/966/looking-for-cubic-bipartite-graphs-with-girth-at-least-six-and-no-cycles-of-len>.
   It is the known Foster-census graph `F038A`, the Levi graph of the
   cyclic `19_3` configuration generated by `{0,1,8}`; see Erskine,
   Griggs, and Širáň (2021), DOI
   <https://doi.org/10.1002/jcd.21773>.

2. **The minimum total order 38 is also prior public information.**  The
   McKay--Afzaly exact extremal table for bipartite `{C4,C8}`-free graphs
   gives maximum edge counts 54 at order 37 and 57 at order 38, with one
   order-38 extremal graph:
   <https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/data/extremal.html>.  Minimum
   degree 3 requires at least `ceil(3n/2)` edges, so this table already
   implies minimum total order 38.  The packet's "previous smallest known
   example: 70" and "new extremal number" language must be withdrawn.

3. **The cycle count is 19 unoriented hexagons, not 38.**  The packet does
   not document the rooted/oriented convention, if any, that produced 38.

4. **The exact graph identification is machine-checked.**  The raw
   checked-out model has SHA-256
   `de21d2eff617a64861387c9ce9e5a3ee0f5d4ccde9f3f9e728f1371b59060b8d`;
   its LF-normalized hash is
   `e6d54bb74d9e35de3939062f2a0d02b2ac6edf3c04064b33549dabc61492022c`.
   The full adjacency-preserving vertex map to Royle's list is in
   `royle_2009_isomorphism.json`.

5. **The computational result that may remain useful is stronger but
   needs different framing.**  Fresh successor runs regenerated and
   `drat-trim`-verified all missing rungs `sigma = 14,...,18`; rungs
   5--13 were independently replayed and `sigma = 4` has the hand
   pair-budget proof.  Together with the hostile encoding audit, this
   certifies the part-size statement: every bipartite graph of minimum
   degree at least 3 with no `C4` and no `C8` has at least 19 vertices in
   **each** bipartition class.  This is stronger than the already-known
   total-order threshold.  Priority for the exact part-size statement is
   not yet cleared.

6. **The fresh large artifacts are not yet a public archive.**  They are
   currently in a local temporary audit directory.  Before any
   reproducibility claim, archive the CNFs, ASCII DRATs, transcripts,
   hashes, generator revision, and checker hash on Zenodo or an equivalent
   durable venue.

7. **The family should not be described as wholly unstudied.**  The exact
   variable-line-size/minimum-degree formulation may be nonstandard, but
   its symmetric 3-uniform subfamily is classical configuration theory.
   Pisanski explicitly described the order-38 object as a configuration
   with triangles but no quadrangles, and the 2021 paper treats it in the
   `v_3` configuration literature.

## What remains unaffected

- The independent graph checker confirms that the known order-38 model is
  simple, connected, cubic, bipartite, `C4`-free and `C8`-free.
- It has explicit `C16` and `C32` cycles and therefore is not a
  counterexample to Erdős problem #64.
- The mathematical graph-to-linear-hypergraph reduction, static `C8`
  clauses, and DoubleLex symmetry breaking survived the independent
  semantic audit.
- This correction does not evaluate or alter the separate two-defect and
  general small-cover lanes except where their public summary relied on
  the withdrawn order-38 novelty claim.
