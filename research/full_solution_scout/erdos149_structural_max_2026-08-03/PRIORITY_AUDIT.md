# Priority and literature audit for Erdős problem #149

Audit date: 2026-08-03

## Exact target

The negative target for the $\Delta=4$ case is a finite simple graph $G$ with

$$
\Delta(G)\le4,\qquad \chi\bigl(L(G)^2\bigr)\ge21.
$$

The Erdős Problems page and the independent UnsolvedMath mirror both marked
problem #149 open when checked.  Database status is not treated as proof of
priority.  Targeted searches were also made for recent proofs, counterexamples,
degree-four examples requiring 21 colours, and public announcements.

## Primary sources checked

* F. R. K. Chung, A. Gyárfás, Z. Tuza, W. T. Trotter,
  [*The maximum number of edges in $2K_2$-free graphs of bounded degree*](https://www.renyi.hu/~gyarfas/Cikkek/48_ChungGyarfasTuzaTrotter_TheMaximumNumberOfEdgesIn2K2FreeGraphsOfBoundedDegree.pdf),
  Discrete Mathematics 81 (1990), 129--135.  Theorem 4 gives at most
  $5D^2/4$ edges for even $D$, with the balanced $C_5$ blow-up as the
  unique equality graph. At $D=4$, the exact threshold is 20 edges.

* M. Huang, M. Santana, G. Yu,
  [*Strong chromatic index of graphs with maximum degree four*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07012),
  Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 25(3) (2018), P3.31,
  [journal record](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v25i3p31).
  This proves the current general upper bound 21 for Δ=4 and explicitly
  leaves the conjectured 20 bound open.

* F. Joos,
  [*Induced Matchings in Graphs of Maximum Degree 4*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.8336),
  later SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics.  This supplies sharp lower
  bounds on induced matching number, but not a 20-colouring theorem.

* A. Kumar, B. Mohar, A. P. Pragada,
  [*An improved bound for the strong clique index of graphs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02698),
  submitted 4 July 2026.  This is the newest directly relevant paper found.
  It improves the asymptotic/general strong-clique coefficient, but its
  integer consequence at Δ=4 does not lower 21 to 20 and it does not resolve
  the strong chromatic-index conjecture.

## Result of priority search

No proof of the Δ=4 upper bound 20, no graph requiring 21 colours, and no
public priority announcement of either was found.  Searches included exact
phrases for "strong chromatic index 21", "maximum degree four", "subquartic",
"counterexample", and Erdős problem 149, with attention to 2026 material.

This is evidence, not a guarantee of novelty.  Before publishing anything
stronger than a finite-order lemma, repeat searches by title/author/citation,
contact a specialist, and have the proof independently refereed.
